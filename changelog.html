Changelog

Release notes for Fasting Tracker. This page is read-only and does not access or change your fasting or weight records.

v1.14.0

27 October 2026
  • Added an in-app changelog so release notes are available from Settings.
  • Release history is also maintained in the repository's CHANGELOG.md.

v1.13.11

27 October 2026
  • Rebuilt stale daily fasting-total caches and prevented incremental cache drift.

v1.13.10

27 October 2026
  • Preserved all affected dates in copied daily-total integrity reports.

v1.13.9

27 October 2026
  • Added a Copy report action and more precise cached-versus-record mismatch details.

v1.13.8

27 October 2026
  • Added affected-date details to daily-total integrity diagnostics and diagnostic exports.

v1.13.7

27 October 2026
  • Recorded sanitized update-stage errors in diagnostics to help distinguish release download, integrity, and staging failures.

v1.13.6

October 2026
  • Improved duration formatting so day-long durations retain their minute component.
  • Added localized daylight-saving/time-zone change notes to daily Timeline, Trend, and Cumulative chart views.

Registro de cambios

Notas de las versiones de Fasting Tracker. Esta página es de solo lectura y no accede ni modifica tus registros de ayuno o peso.

27 de octubre de 2026

v1.13.10

  • Se conservaron todas las fechas afectadas en los informes copiados de integridad de totales diarios.

v1.13.9

27 de octubre de 2026

v1.13.7

27 de octubre de 2026
  • Los diagnósticos registran errores de preparación de actualización saneados para ayudar a distinguir problemas de descarga, integridad y preparación de archivos.

v1.13.6

octubre de 2026
